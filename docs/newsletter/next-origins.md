## Measured on the same samples, synthetic vent minerals conduct electrons tens of thousands of times better than ions

One idea for how life began places it at alkaline vents on the early sea floor, where electrons crossing thin mineral walls could turn carbon dioxide into organic molecules. This team found electrons moved easily through synthetic vent minerals but ions moved far more slowly, so ions, not electrons, may set the pace.

https://doi.org/10.1039/d6cc04072a


## In a model with measured rates, a copy with an early mistake slows down, so short copying windows repeated in cycles could make exact copies of strands up to about 50 letters

Before enzymes, RNA had to be copied by chemistry alone, which gets roughly one letter in ten wrong, but one mistake tends to bring more and slows the copy right down. So if copying runs only briefly, the copies that finish are mostly the clean ones, and in this model warm and cool cycles that keep restarting the copying could make exact copies of strands up to about 50 letters long.

https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.72856
