## Measured on the same samples, synthetic vent minerals conduct electrons tens of thousands of times better than ions

One idea for how life began places it at alkaline vents on the early sea floor, where electrons crossing thin mineral walls could turn carbon dioxide into organic molecules. This team found electrons moved easily through synthetic vent minerals but ions moved far more slowly, so ions, not electrons, may set the pace.
