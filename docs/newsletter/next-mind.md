## A network measure of alpha coherence picked out patients who regained higher cognition better than a standard connectivity measure

After a severe brain injury, some patients cannot show that they are aware, and doctors often read the alpha rhythm in a scalp EEG to judge how much of the brain is working. In 95 clinical recordings from two hospitals, alpha that stayed in step across the back of the brain went with recovery of higher thinking.
