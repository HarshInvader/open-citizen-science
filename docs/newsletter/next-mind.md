## A network measure of alpha coherence picked out patients who regained higher cognition better than a standard connectivity measure

After a severe brain injury, some patients cannot show that they are aware, and doctors often read the alpha rhythm in a scalp EEG to judge how much of the brain is working. In 95 clinical recordings from two hospitals, alpha that stayed in step across the back of the brain went with recovery of higher thinking.

https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cortex.2026.11.009


## Phi from integrated information theory, computed on four-part systems built from rodent spikes, dropped in NREM sleep; in visual cortex the drop went away once firing rates were matched

Integrated information theory says consciousness is a system's tightly joined cause and effect, and this study measured it on tiny four-part systems built from neurons in sleeping and waking mice and rats; it fell in deep sleep, most of all when neurons went quiet. But neurons also fire less in deep sleep, and once firing was evened out the drop in mouse visual cortex disappeared, while the one in rat frontal cortex stayed.

https://doi.org/10.1093/nc/niag077
